Living here 📱

Practical tips to settle in quickly.

SIM & Connectivity

  • Major providers: EE, O2, Vodafone, Three. Buy a pay-as-you-go SIM at the airport or local shop.
  • You'll need ID; some providers allow online activation with passport photos.

Money

  • Contactless payments are widely accepted. Notify your bank of travel to avoid card blocks.
  • Cash: keep a small amount for taxis or small shops.

Food & cultural tips

  • Indian groceries are available in many cities; search for local stores or order delivery.
  • Explore local markets for fresh produce and affordable meals.
