Before you land ✈️

What to prepare in India so your arrival is smooth.

Essentials to pack

  • Passport, printed visa (and digital copies).
  • One or two sets of climate-appropriate clothes (layering helps).
  • Phone charger, adapters, powerbank.

Order ahead (optional)

Order Indian groceries or basic supplies to arrive at your hotel so you have familiar food on arrival. This is optional and only if you prefer not to carry heavy items.

Documents & money

  • Carry a printed address for your first-night stay.
  • Bring a debit/credit card and some GBP cash for taxi and small purchases.

Phone & connectivity

Unlock your phone if possible; alternatively buy a local SIM after landing — we explain how on the "Living here" page.
