If something's wrong 🆘

Emergency guidance and next steps if you face problems.

Lost passport or documents

  • Contact your nearest consulate/embassy for emergency travel documents.
  • Report a lost passport to the local police and keep a copy of the report.

Health & NHS

  • For emergencies, call 999. For non-emergency medical help, visit a local GP or urgent care centre.
  • If you need hospital care, NHS services are available — bring ID and explain your status.

Local support

  • Keep emergency numbers handy and share them with your group.
  • Contact your accommodation if you need help with local language, police, or transport.
