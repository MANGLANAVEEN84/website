Getting here 🚌

Options from major London airports to central locations.

From Heathrow (LHR)

  • Heathrow Express: Fastest to Paddington — ~15–20 minutes. Cost: ~£30–£35.
  • Elizabeth Line: Slower but cheaper — ~30 minutes, cheaper fares.
  • Taxi / Ride-hailing: Door-to-door, cost varies (~£50+ depending on traffic).

From Gatwick (LGW)

  • Gatwick Express: To Victoria — ~30 minutes.
  • Thameslink / Southern: Cheaper options into central London.

Tips

  • Allow extra time for immigration and baggage collection.
  • Use contactless payment (cards or phone) for most trains and buses.
  • If you need help with luggage, ask airport staff — they're used to assisting visitors.
